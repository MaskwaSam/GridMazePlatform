How to read the catalog

Open sides are entrances

North is shown at the top of each plan diagram. A dark edge is a tall wall. A green side label is open, so a ball, robot, or player can travel through that side. Rotate any printed piece to obtain the other directions.

Red-tinted square: common platform
Dark bar: wall copied from the Rhino source
Green label: open connection

The diagrams explain connectivity; the rendered images show the printable geometry. The Rhino model—not the photograph—is the dimensional source.

Rhino-derived wall set

The five maze layouts, now in both H2D sizes

These pieces reuse the wall height, thickness, transitions, and edge profile from the working Rhino model. For a 300 mm wall, the two designed end regions remain unchanged and only the uniform middle is extended by 100 mm. The wall profile is never enlarged or stretched. The four-way hub is the deliberate exception at the wall foot: it uses only the source wall's clean 1.2 mm vertical panel, without the triangular sloped reinforcement corners.

Shared connection rule: every rectangular piece below retains one centred X1C connector pocket on each side, including sides that carry a wall. Open-side labels describe maze travel. The widening transition has connectors only at its two open ends; the four-way 45° hub has one centred connector and a 200 mm opening on every side.

H2D printing

Keep the piece flat and preserve scale

Import every file at 100% scale in millimetres and place the platform flat on the build plate. The 200 mm pieces fit both H2D envelopes. Orient the 200 × 300 adapter with its 300 mm dimension along the 320 mm bed axis. For practical edge margin, use the 300 × 300 adapter in the single-nozzle 325 × 320 × 325 mm envelope; the dual-nozzle envelope is exactly 300 mm wide and leaves no nominal side margin.

The connector fit remains a geometry and material calibration question even on the new printer. Print a connector or edge-pocket coupon before committing to several full platforms, and confirm the selected plate's excluded zones and adhesion allowance in Bambu Studio before printing the 300 mm parts.
